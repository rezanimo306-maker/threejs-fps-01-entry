EMBERLINE

A round-based tactical FPS. You are EMBER — five attackers against five defenders on the dusk-lit Foundry Yard. Plant the spike at Reactor (A) or Crane Dock (B), or eliminate the whole defending team. Win rounds, earn money, buy better guns.

W A S D move  ·  Mouse aim  ·  Click fire  ·  R reload
Shift walk quietly  ·  Ctrl / C crouch  ·  1–5 switch weapon  ·  G throw grenade
E plant / defuse the spike  ·  B buy menu (freeze time)  ·  Right-click scope (Longbow)
Space jump

Stop to aim: firing on the move sprays wide, crouching steadies you, headshots deal double. Bots run routes, hold angles and react to gunfire. First team to 5 rounds takes the match.

Best on desktop Chrome · WebGL 2 · no downloads, no network — everything runs from this page.
